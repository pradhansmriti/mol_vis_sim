Starting choice: CHARMM36 protein topology — select your local top_all36_prot.rtf. Matching protein parameters in our distribution: par_all36m_prot.prm (CHARMM36m). Files are not bundled or downloaded automatically; other compatible topologies can be selected. CHARMM36 has separate protein and lipid topology files; older combined files such as top_all27_prot_lipid.inp contain CHARMM22 proteins / CHARMM27 lipids. We use only the protein topology here.
Upload matching files to check parameter coverage with the chosen solvent in OpenMM.

Protein-only selection is applied before psfgen: waters, ions and other non-protein components are excluded and recorded. Choose whether to retain input hydrogens or rebuild them with the selected topology. Rebuilding avoids input hydrogen naming mismatches; review protonation choices first. Resolve chain gaps, alternate positions and custom/disulfide patches first. HSE/HSD/HSP choices apply to residues named HIS; explicitly named variants remain unchanged. HIS and termini use your selections; other residues use their topology-defined states. Solvation and salt are chosen later, after reviewing the prepared structure. Site-specific or unusual protonation requires external preparation and pair upload. No minimization or solvation is performed.

Later: solvation, salt and OpenMM compatibility
First prepare the protein and review its structure. Open these settings when you are ready to choose the simulation environment.
Default 0.2 M ionic screening for implicit OBC2/GBn2; no discrete ions are added. These are OpenMM GB models, not NAMD GBIS.
Select your local toppar_water_ions.str. Only TIP3 water is added; Na+, Cl- and neutralization are disabled.
Protonation comes from step 1 or your uploaded PSF. Solvent selection does not change the prepared protonation states. Explicit water builds a cubic periodic box with PME (1.2 nm cutoff, switching from 1.0 nm). No Na+, Cl- or counterions are added. The protein charge remains unchanged.
Readiness testing allows 20 minimizer iterations per optimizer pass (constraints may require more passes), then runs the selected 1–1,000 NVT steps at your temperature and timestep. Checkpoints are saved every 100 steps. This is a short execution test, not equilibration or production.
Solvation and salt are deferred until after structure review.

Output intervals · next run
Choose before starting a run. Every N steps means N × the integration timestep: at 2 fs, 10 steps = 20 fs (0.02 ps). These intervals control actual dynamics measurements and coordinate snapshots, independent of computer speed. Step 0 is included. Minimization uses optimizer reports, not simulation timesteps. The run record retains the latest Cα coordinates. Output intervals longer than the selected test length produce only an initial output.
